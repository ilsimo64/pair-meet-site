Community safety

Meet new people. Stay in control.

Pair Meet provides tools to help participants protect their experience and report behavior that breaks the rules.

🛑 Block users

Use the available block controls to prevent unwanted interactions with specific users.

🚩 Report misconduct

Report users or messages that violate the rules so the relevant moderation team can review the incident.

🕶️ Incognito mode

Use /incognito to hide your profile information in supported Pair Meet views.

🔒 Server moderation

Server administrators can configure a report channel and custom blocked-word filters.

🔗 Link protection

Pair Meet includes link and media restrictions intended to reduce abuse in new conversations.

⚖️ Network enforcement

Moderation actions, including temporary or permanent network bans, may be applied to protect the community.

Keep conversations respectful

Do not harass, threaten, exploit, impersonate, spam or share illegal content. Never share personal information you do not want other participants to know. If a conversation makes you uncomfortable, leave it and use the block or report tools.

For assistance or to appeal a moderation action, visit the Pair Meet Support Server.
