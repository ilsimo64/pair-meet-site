Privacy Policy.

Privacy information for Pair Meet. The technical notes below are limited to what can be confirmed from the supplied source code; operator-specific disclosures still need to be completed.

Controls available in the bot

  • /deletemydata is provided to request deletion of the data Pair Meet stored about you.
  • /setbio stores a profile biography of up to 300 characters; leaving the option empty clears it.
  • /incognito toggles anonymous mode and hides the participant’s profile in the profile-view flow.
  • /private lets a server opt out of the public /servers listing.

Chat and moderation data

The source includes storage and handling for chat sessions, message relay metadata, blocks, friends, language preferences, reports, server bans and custom filtered words. The exact retention periods and backup lifecycle are not specified here.

What the operator must disclose

Before publishing this policy as final, the operator must identify the data controller, contact details, hosting and database providers, purposes and legal bases, retention periods, international transfers (if any), user rights and the process for exercising them.

Questions or deletion requests

Use /deletemydata for the bot’s in-product deletion flow. For questions, contact the operator through the support server.

This page deliberately does not invent a company identity, retention schedule or legal basis. Complete those details with the actual deployment and operator information before relying on it as a formal privacy notice.
