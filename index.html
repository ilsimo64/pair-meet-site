Meet new people across Discord without leaving your server.

Pair Meet connects users from different communities in private, moderated chat rooms. Match at random, search by shared interests, or invite friends with a 4-digit code.

🎲 Random & Interest Matching

Start a spontaneous conversation with anyone on the network or find people who share your hobbies using /find.

🔑 Private Group Rooms

Every session creates a private temporary channel with a 4-digit code so friends can request to join (up to 6 participants).

🕵️ Incognito Mode

Keep your profile private. Incognito mode hides your username and avatar and prevents same-server matches.

🛡️ Built-in Safety & Moderation

Protected by Discord AutoMod integration, progressive link-unlock protection, custom server word filters, and instant reporting.

⚡ Real-Time Sync

Seamless cross-server message relay supporting replies, attachments, edits, deletions, and reactions.

🛠️ Guided 1-Minute Setup

Server admins can configure panels, chat categories, and moderation logs effortlessly with /setup.
